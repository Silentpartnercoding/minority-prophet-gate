# Code of conduct

Participation requires respectful, evidence-led collaboration. Discuss claims
and artifacts rather than attacking people. Credit prior work, disclose
material conflicts, and do not manufacture identities, endorsements, evidence,
or apparent independent verification.

Harassment, intimidation, discriminatory conduct, doxxing, and publication of
secrets or sensitive vulnerability details are not acceptable. Maintainers may
remove material or restrict participation to protect contributors and the
project. Sensitive reports should use GitHub's private security-advisory
interface.
